# Project Dyadyami: Luxury Immersive Transformation

## Vision
Transforming a standard travel booking platform into a "Sensational Quiet Luxury" experience (Aman x Christie's level).

## Key Components
- **Globe.tsx**: Uses custom shaders for atmospheric glow.
- **Wizard**: GSAP & Framer Motion for cinematic transitions.
- **Next.js 15**: PPR enabled in `next.config.js`.

## Roadmap
- P0: Atmospheric Shaders, Smooth Transitions, Next.js 15 Optimization.
- P1: Ambient Sound, 4K Video Integration, Booking Logic.
