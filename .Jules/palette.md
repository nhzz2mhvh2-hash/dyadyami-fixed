## 2025-05-14 - Semantic Progress Indicators in Wizards
**Learning:** In highly customized "luxury" interfaces, semantic markup like `<nav>` and `<ol>` is often sacrificed for visual control. Restoring these while maintaining the aesthetic requires careful positioning (e.g., keeping decorative lines outside the `<ol>` to maintain HTML validity).
**Action:** Use `<nav>` and `<ol>` for all multi-step flows, ensuring decorative elements are properly nested as siblings to the list, not children.
