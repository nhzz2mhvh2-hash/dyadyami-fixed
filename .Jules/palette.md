## 2026-06-30 - Accessible Progress Indicators in Quiet Luxury Design
**Learning:** In minimalist "quiet luxury" designs, interactive wizards often implement visually custom progress indicators (e.g., bare dots or names) that bypass default browser styles for alignment. This typically results in standard keyboard-navigable focus indicators being removed, raw `onClick` handlers attached to structural tags (like `div`), and unvisited future steps lacking disabled affordances. Elevating these components to semantic `<nav>` and `<ol>` layouts with `<button type="button">` elements equipped with proper `aria-current` and custom focus indicators preserves design style while ensuring complete keyboard accessibility and screen-reader semantics.
**Action:** Always refactor custom progress trackers into fully semantic `<nav aria-label="Progress">` structures with numbered/labelled `<button type="button">` nodes. Use group focus rings with high visual offsets to preserve visual appeal while providing strong keyboard focus visual support.
