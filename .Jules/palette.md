## 2026-06-30 - Accessible Interactive Header Navigation
**Learning:** Brand text and logo elements in luxury site headers are frequently styled as static text divs, preventing keyboard users from returning home and creating screen reader gaps.
**Action:** Wrap header brand identity elements in semantic `<a>` links with clear `aria-label`s and focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-gold`) to ensure both keyboard navigation and brand aesthetics are preserved.
