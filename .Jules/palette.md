## 2026-06-30 - Accessible "Quiet Luxury" Progress Indicators
**Learning:** In minimalist "quiet luxury" interfaces, interactive elements like progress dots often lack standard affordances (borders, backgrounds) to maintain a clean aesthetic. Converting these to semantic `<nav>` and `<button>` elements ensures they are discoverable by screen readers and keyboard users without disrupting the visual 'void' design. High-offset focus rings (`ring-offset-4`) provide clear affordance that feels like a premium design layer rather than a browser default.
**Action:** Always wrap visual progress indicators in semantic `<button>` elements with dynamic `aria-label` and `aria-current` attributes. Use `focus-visible` with generous offsets to maintain aesthetic breathing room while meeting accessibility standards.
