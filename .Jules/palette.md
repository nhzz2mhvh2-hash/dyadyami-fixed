## 2026-06-30 - Semantic Wizard Progress Navigation
**Learning:** For multi-step wizard flows, visual progress indicators (like progress dots) are often not keyboard-accessible and lack semantic structure for assistive technologies. Wrapping them in a semantic `<nav>` and `<ol>` block, and implementing them as accessible `<button type="button">` elements with explicit `aria-label`, `aria-current`, and proper focus states, ensures keyboard and screen reader accessibility while maintaining the luxury aesthetic.
**Action:** When creating multi-step processes or Wizards, use semantic HTML tags (`<nav>`, `<ol>`, `<li>`), explicit accessible buttons rather than click handlers on plain list items, and manage disabled states so that backtracking is permitted while future unvisited steps are properly disabled.
